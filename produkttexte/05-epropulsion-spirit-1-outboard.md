### Crafted for Convenience
The Spirit 1.0 is designed to make life on the water simpler. Lightweight yet powerful, it’s easy to carry, transport and store, giving you the freedom to take it wherever your adventures lead. Its compact design makes it a versatile solution for a wide range of marine adventures, ideal for boaters who value convenience without compromising performance.
### Innovative Battery Technology
Powered by a lightweight 8.7 kg lithium-ion battery with a 1,276 Wh capacity, the Spirit 1.0 is built to perform in all conditions. Engineered for durability, the buoyant battery even floats, giving you peace of mind on the water. With enough energy for a full day’s adventure, you can easily swap the integrated battery for extended range, or recharge on the go with solar power while you cruise.
### Remote Control Capability
**Spirit 1.0 Evo Remote** is the only sub-5HP** electric outboard on the market offering remote helm steering, with a choice of Evo top-mount or side-mount controls.

### Hydrogeneration
The **Spirit 1.0 Evo** is the industry’s first electric outboard to feature hydrogeneration technology, allowing the battery to recharge while the vessel is under sail. This breakthrough maximises energy efficiency and extends range without dependence on shore power. It represents a major advancement in marine technology, uniting high-performance propulsion with sustainable energy management.
*   The Spirit 1.0 Evo outboard is capable of driving the propeller to recharge the ePropulsion battery through water flow when specific operating conditions are met (see the user manual in the download centre for detailed requirements).
